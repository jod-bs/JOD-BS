Are Your Google Ads Bringing Business or Just Clicks?

Running a campaign does not automatically mean your advertising is working.

You may be dealing with:

High ad spend with very few enquiries

Clicks coming from people who are unlikely to become customers

Keywords attracting irrelevant searches

Ads that receive impressions but fail to generate action

Landing pages that do not support the promise made in the ad

Campaigns using the same strategy for every audience

Poor visibility into which campaigns actually generate leads

Conversion tracking that does not give you a clear picture of performance

When these problems are not addressed, increasing the budget usually means spending more without fixing the underlying issue.
Effective paid advertising starts with understanding where the money is going and what happens after someone clicks.
