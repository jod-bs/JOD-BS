The Content Problem

Is Your Content Actually Helping Your Business?

Content can take a lot of time to produce. But if your audience is not finding it, understanding it, or acting on it, publishing more may not solve the problem.

You may be dealing with:

Website pages that explain your services but fail to communicate their value

Blog posts that receive little meaningful traffic

Content created around keywords without understanding search intent

Topics that do not match what your customers actually want to know

Different teams creating content without a consistent brand voice

Is your website still showcasing outdated content that no longer represents your business? It’s time for a refresh!

Social and website content that communicate different messages

Content that gets attention but does not support enquiries or conversions

Good content starts with a simple question: What does your audience need to understand before they are ready to choose your business?

The Customer Journey

Content Should Move With the Customer

Not everyone who sees your content is ready to buy. Someone may first find your brand through a search. Another person may find a social post. Someone else may visit your website after hearing about your business.

Their questions are different at each stage.

01 Stage 01

When They Are Discovering

They need useful information that helps them understand a problem, topic, or opportunity.

02 Stage 02

When They Are Considering

They want deeper information about possible solutions, approaches, features, benefits, and alternatives.

03 Stage 03

When They Are Deciding

They need clear service information, proof, answers to concerns, and a reason to choose one business over another.

Grow your brand with better content. Your content should support these different moments rather than trying to sell something in every piece.
